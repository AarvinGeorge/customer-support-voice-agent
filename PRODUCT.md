# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People at companies that use Quillstack, a team docs and knowledge-base product sold to businesses. Three roles: the workspace **owner** (ops or IT lead who signed the contract), the **billing admin** (finance or operations), and the **member** (an employee using the product daily). They arrive with a specific problem: a charge they do not recognise, a refund, a login or SSO error, a how-to question. They want a correct answer or a done action in minutes, by chat at a desk or by voice when that is faster. A second audience matters for this demo: technical reviewers evaluating the agent, who use the observability drawer to see why the agent did what it did.

## Product Purpose

A customer support agent that resolves routine billing, account and troubleshooting requests correctly, by chat or voice, and hands everything else to a human with a complete ticket. Answers come only from Quillstack's published help articles, with citations; actions (refunds, tickets) follow code-enforced policy and need the customer's explicit yes. Success: verified resolution without a human, zero made-up policy, every unsure case escalated, and a visible trail of each step, check and decision.

## Brand Personality

**Calm, precise, transparent.** The voice is a capable colleague, not a chatbot: short sentences, plain words, no exclamation marks, no filler apologies. It says what it is doing while it does it, says what it does not know, and quotes the policy when it says no. Emotional goal: the customer feels in safe hands; the reviewer feels nothing is hidden.

References: the Claude and ChatGPT apps (calm conversation surface, expandable reasoning and tool steps, minimal chrome) and ElevenLabs and OpenAI voice mode (the orb as the focal point in voice, soft ambient motion).

## Anti-references

- **Generic chatbot widget:** bubbly support-widget look, cartoon avatars, emoji-heavy replies.
- **Neon AI aesthetic:** purple-blue gradients, glowing glass panels, sci-fi dark mode by default.
- **Cluttered enterprise console:** dense grey dashboards, tables everywhere, tiny text.
- **SaaS landing-page tropes:** hero metrics, gradient text, identical card grids.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, quietly.** Every step, gate and decision is available one click away (collapsed step trail, drawer), never shouting for attention.
2. **The conversation is the product.** Chrome recedes; content, citations and confirmations carry the page.
3. **Nothing irreversible without a clear yes.** Confirmations are explicit, legible and never hidden in prose.
4. **Honest states.** Waiting, checking, failing and escalating each look distinct and say plainly what is happening and what did or did not change.
5. **Voice is a mode, not a separate product.** The same thread, the same steps, the same memory; the orb only changes how it is heard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, full keyboard operation, visible focus, logical reading order, screen-reader status text for streaming steps and voice states. Reduced motion respected everywhere, including the orb and waveform (static or minimal fallback). Voice states always carry a text label, never color alone. Captions are always on in voice mode. Long IDs shown in full in text even when spoken in short form.
