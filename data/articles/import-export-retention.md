This article explains how to bring content into Quillstack, how to take it out, and what happens to your data when it is deleted or when a subscription ends.

## Importing content

You can import existing docs into a Quillstack workspace:

- **Markdown files:** import single files or a zip of folders. Folders become nested pages.
- **HTML files:** headings, lists, tables and links are kept.
- **CSV files:** each row becomes an entry in a Quillstack table.

To import, open Settings, then Import, choose the file type and upload your files. Large imports run in the background and you get a notification when they finish.

### Tips for a clean import

- Use one top-level folder per team or space so the page tree stays tidy.
- Check a few imported pages before you share the workspace with everyone.
- Links between imported pages are updated to point at the new Quillstack pages where possible.

## Exporting content

- **A single page:** open the page menu and choose Export, then Markdown or PDF.
- **The whole workspace:** an account owner can export everything under Settings, then Export. The export is a zip file of Markdown and attached files, and a download link is emailed to the owner when it is ready.

Exports include pages you have access to. Private pages of other people are not included in a page export.

## Deleted pages

When you delete a page, it moves to Trash. You can restore it from Trash in the sidebar.

Removing a page from Trash deletes it permanently. It cannot be restored after that.

## When a subscription ends

If your subscription is cancelled or does not renew, we recommend an owner exports the workspace before the end of the current term. Contract statuses are explained in Plans, pricing and contracts.

Enterprise data handling after a contract ends follows the terms of your contract.

## Deleting workspace data

Deleting all of a workspace's data cannot be undone, so it is never done automatically by the support assistant. An account owner can ask support to delete workspace data, and we open a ticket for our account team, who confirm the request with the owner before anything is deleted.
