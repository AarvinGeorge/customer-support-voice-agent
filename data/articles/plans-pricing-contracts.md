This article explains the four Quillstack plans, what each one costs, how contracts and renewals work, and what each contract status means.

## Plans at a glance

Quillstack has four plans. Every plan includes the core workspace: pages, docs, search and comments. The plans differ in team size, how you pay, and which admin features are included.

- **Free:** for individuals and small teams trying Quillstack.
- **Pro:** for small teams, typically 3 to 30 seats.
- **Business:** for mid-size teams, typically 30 to 300 seats. Includes single sign-on (SSO).
- **Enterprise:** for organizations with 300 or more seats, on a custom contract. Includes SSO.

A seat is one person in your workspace.

## Pricing

- **Free** costs nothing and has no payment method on file.
- **Pro** is $8 per seat per month, paid by card and billed monthly.
- **Business** is $16 per seat per month, billed annually by invoice.
- **Enterprise** pricing is set in a custom contract.

## Typical totals

These examples show what a typical team pays:

- Pro with 3 to 30 seats: $24 to $240 a month.
- Business with 30 to 300 seats: $5,760 to $57,600 a year.

Enterprise totals depend on the contract.

## Free plan

The Free plan has no charges. Because nothing is billed, there are no invoices, no refunds and no billing contact to manage on Free. Billing actions such as refunds or billing contact changes are not available on this plan.

If your team needs more, you can move to a paid plan. Plan changes are handled by our account team: ask support and we will open a request for you.

## Annual terms and renewals

- **Pro** runs month to month. Each month is charged to the card on file.
- **Business** runs on an annual term. The invoice for the full term is issued at the start of the term and sent to the billing contact. The term renews automatically at the end unless notice has been given.
- **Enterprise** terms, renewal dates and notice rules are set in your contract.

## Contract statuses

Every account has one contract status. Support uses it to decide which requests are possible.

- **Trialing:** the account is on a trial. Nothing has been charged yet.
- **Active:** the account is in good standing and normal rules apply.
- **Past due:** a payment failed or an invoice is unpaid. Refunds are not available while an account is past due. See Billing, invoices and payments for what to do next.
- **In notice:** notice has been given to cancel or not renew. The workspace stays active until the end of the current term, and normal rules apply until then.
- **Cancelled:** the subscription has ended. A recent charge can still be refunded under the normal refund policy.

## Changing plans or cancelling

The support assistant cannot change your plan, cancel your subscription, delete data, change the account owner or change your payment method. These changes are made by our account team, so a person always reviews them.

To ask for one of these changes, contact support and say what you need. We will open a ticket for the account team and give you the ticket number so you can check its status later.
