This article explains the notification settings each person controls, how your support PIN is used, and which account settings need our account team.

## Notification preferences

Each person chooses which emails they receive from Quillstack. Your choices apply only to you; they do not change anyone else's settings.

There are four types of notification:

- **Product updates:** news about new features and improvements.
- **Billing notices:** messages about charges, invoices and payments.
- **Incident alerts:** notices when a Quillstack service has a problem that may affect you.
- **Weekly digest:** a weekly summary of activity in your workspace.

You can turn each type on or off in Quillstack under Settings, then Notifications.

## Your support PIN

Each person has their own 4-digit support PIN. Support uses your account ID and your PIN to confirm who you are before it looks at your account or makes any change.

- In chat, enter your PIN only in the secure verification box. Never type it into a chat message.
- In a voice conversation, say your PIN only when support asks you to verify.
- If verification fails 3 times in one conversation, support stops asking and opens a ticket so a person can help you.

## Account settings support can help with

After you verify, support can:

- answer questions about your account, plan and contract status
- look up your own tickets and their status
- open tickets for anything that needs a person

Owners and billing admins can also ask about charges, invoices, refunds and the billing contact. See Roles and permissions.

## Settings our account team changes

Some changes always go to our account team, so a person reviews them:

- the account owner
- the plan, or cancelling the subscription
- the payment method
- the invoice company name, billing address and tax ID
- deleting workspace data

Ask support and we will open a request for you.
