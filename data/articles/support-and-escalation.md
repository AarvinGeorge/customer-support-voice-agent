This article explains how to reach Quillstack support, how fast we respond on each plan, how tickets move, and when a person from our team takes over.

## How to reach support

You can talk to the Quillstack support assistant by chat or by voice, and switch between them in the same conversation without starting again.

To look at your account, make a change or open a ticket, support first verifies you with your account ID and your 4-digit support PIN, so our team knows who to follow up with. Before you verify, support can answer general questions from public help articles.

If you cannot verify, for example because you have forgotten your support PIN or verification is locked after too many tries, support cannot open a ticket for you in that conversation. You can start a new conversation and verify there once you have your PIN; after 5 failed attempts on an account, verification for that account is paused for 15 minutes.

## Priorities and response targets

Every ticket gets a priority from your plan. The first-response target is the time within which our team aims to send a first reply.

| Plan | Priority | First-response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise, blocking issue | P1 | 1 hour |
| Enterprise | P2 | 4 hours |
| Business | P2 | 8 hours |
| Pro | P3 | 24 hours |
| Free | P4 | 72 hours |

## Tickets

Each ticket goes to one queue: billing, technical, account or approvals.

A ticket moves through these stages:

- **Open:** the ticket has been created and is waiting for our team.
- **Pending approval:** the ticket carries a prepared request, such as a refund, that a person must approve or reject.
- **Resolved:** the request was completed, the approval was decided, or the question was answered.
- **Closed:** the ticket is final.

You can ask support "what's the status of my ticket?" at any time. In voice, support reads a ticket by its last four digits, for example "ticket ending 8213".

Limits keep the queue fair:

- A verified account can open up to 5 tickets per day.
- If you already have an open ticket in the same category, support adds a note to it instead of opening a duplicate.
- Every ticket belongs to a verified person, so support verifies you before it opens one.

## Approvals

Some requests are always decided by a person on our team. Support prepares the request, asks whether you want to send it for approval, and on your yes opens a Pending approval ticket.

These requests need approval:

- every Business refund
- Pro refunds over $150, refunds up to 7 days after the refund window, and a second refund within 90 days
- every Enterprise refund, billing contact change and grace extension

The approver checks the request against the same rules again before anything changes. See Refund policy for the full rules.

## When a person takes over

Support opens a ticket for our team when:

- you ask to speak to a person
- your question is about Quillstack but our help center does not cover it
- the answer is still unclear after one clarifying question
- you ask for a discount, a roadmap date, a legal answer or a security commitment
- something fails twice while support is working on your request

When something fails, support tells you plainly what did and did not change.

## How to talk to a person

To reach a human support agent, ask to speak to a person, in chat or by voice. Support opens a ticket for our team and gives you its number, or adds a note to your open ticket in the same category.

## What support will not promise

Support answers only from our published policies and your account records. It does not offer discounts, policy exceptions, roadmap dates, or legal or security commitments. Those always go to our team.
