This article covers how Quillstack bills each plan, how to find charges and invoices, the billing contact, failed payments, past-due accounts and payment grace extensions.

## How billing works

- **Free:** no billing.
- **Pro:** charged monthly to a card, based on the number of seats.
- **Business:** billed annually by invoice at the start of each term.
- **Enterprise:** billed as set out in your contract.

## Viewing charges and invoices

Account owners and billing admins can see the account's charges and invoices. Members cannot see billing amounts.

Each charge in our billing record shows:

- a charge ID
- the date
- the amount
- a description
- the status, for example paid or failed
- whether it has been refunded

You can ask support about a specific invoice or charge by its ID, for example INV-2026-0412. In a voice conversation you can say the last four digits, such as "the invoice ending 0412". We only look up IDs within your own account.

## Invoices

Business invoices are issued at the start of each annual term and sent to the billing contact. Enterprise invoices follow your contract.

The company name, billing address and tax ID on an invoice are changed by our account team. Ask support and we will open a request for you.

## Billing contact

The billing contact email is where invoices and billing notices are sent.

- Only an account owner or billing admin can ask to change it.
- Because changing where invoices go is a common route for fraud, we confirm your identity again with your support PIN before the change is made.
- The billing contact can be changed at most once per day for each account.
- On Enterprise, a member of our team approves every billing contact change.

Billing contact changes are not available on the Free plan, because Free has no billing.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, or an invoice is not paid on time, the account becomes past due. A failed payment was never collected, so there is nothing to refund for it.

Payment method changes are made by our account team for security. Ask support and we will open a request for you.

## Past-due accounts

While an account is past due:

- Refunds are not available. Any refund request is passed to our billing team.
- An owner or billing admin can ask for a payment grace extension, described below.
- The status stays past due until the balance is paid.

## Grace extension

A payment grace extension gives a past-due account more time to pay. It is available only to account owners and billing admins, and only while the account is past due.

- **Pro:** an extension of 7 days, once per 12 months.
- **Business:** an extension of 14 days, once per 6 months.
- **Enterprise:** extension requests are reviewed by our team.
- **Free:** not available, because Free has no billing.

An extension sets a new date by which payment is due. It does not change the contract status: the account stays past due, and refunds stay unavailable, until the balance is paid.

To ask for an extension, contact support.

## Changes support cannot make here

These changes are made by our account team, not by the support assistant:

- cancelling the subscription or changing the plan
- changing the payment method
- changing the account owner
- changing the invoice company name, billing address or tax ID

Contact support and we will open a ticket for the account team.
