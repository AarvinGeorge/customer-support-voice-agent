Every person in a Quillstack workspace has one of three roles. The role decides what that person can see and what they can ask support to do.

## The three roles

- **Owner:** usually the operations lead, IT lead or founder who signed the contract. Owners manage plan and contract questions, admin setup and SSO.
- **Billing admin:** usually someone in finance or operations. Billing admins handle charges, refunds, invoices and failed payments.
- **Member:** an employee who uses Quillstack every day. Members write and read docs and ask how-to questions.

For billing, owners and billing admins have exactly the same permissions.

## Billing permissions

Only an account owner or billing admin can make billing changes. This includes:

- seeing charges, invoices and the billing contact
- asking for a refund
- asking to change the billing contact email
- asking for a payment grace extension

If a member asks for any of these, support explains that the request needs an owner or billing admin and can open a ticket so the right person follows up.

## What members can do

Members can:

- get answers from the help center
- open support tickets and check the status of their own tickets
- change their own notification preferences

Members cannot see billing amounts, invoices or the billing contact.

## How support knows your role

Support identifies you as a person, not just as an account. You verify with your account ID and your own 4-digit support PIN, and support then uses the role on your profile. This is why two people at the same company can get different answers to the same billing request. Before you verify, support can only give general answers from public help articles.

## Changing roles or the account owner

Changing someone's role or the account owner is handled by our account team. Ask support and we will open a request for you.
