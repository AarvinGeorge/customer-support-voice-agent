This article explains when Quillstack refunds a charge, who can ask for a refund, and what happens after you ask. Support applies these rules exactly as written here and cannot make exceptions to them.

## Refunds at a glance

- **Free:** nothing is charged, so there is nothing to refund.
- **Pro:** a refund of up to $150 within 14 days of the charge can be issued as soon as you confirm. Larger, later or repeat refunds go to our team for approval.
- **Business:** every refund within 30 days of the charge goes to our team for approval.
- **Enterprise:** every refund is reviewed by our team under the terms of your contract.
- **Past-due accounts:** refunds are not available while the account has an unpaid balance.

## Who can ask for a refund

Only an account owner or a billing admin can ask for a refund. Members cannot request refunds or see billing amounts. See Roles and permissions for details.

A refund is always for the amount shown on the charge in our billing record. You choose which charge; you do not choose the amount.

## How refunds work

1. Tell us which charge you mean, for example by its invoice ID or the date and amount.
2. We check the charge against the rules in this article.
3. If the refund can be issued right away, we read back the amount and the charge date and ask you to confirm. Nothing changes until you say yes.
4. If the refund needs approval, we ask whether you would like to send it to our team. If you say yes, we open a Pending approval ticket and give you its number.
5. Once a refund is issued, it goes back to the original payment method. The time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank.

Some charges cannot be refunded:

- A charge that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again.
- A charge that was never collected, for example a failed payment, cannot be refunded.

## Free plan

The Free plan has no charges, so there is nothing to refund.

## Trials

Trials are not charged, so there is nothing to refund during a trial.

## Pro plan refunds

### Refunds we can issue right away

A Pro refund is issued as soon as you confirm when all of these are true:

- You ask within 14 days of the charge date.
- The amount is up to $150.
- It is your account's first refund in 90 days.
- The account is not past due.

### Refunds that need approval

These Pro refunds go to our team for approval:

- A charge over $150.
- A charge up to 7 days after the refund window has closed.
- A second refund within 90 days of a previous refund.

### Refunds we cannot make

A Pro charge more than 7 days after the refund window has closed cannot be refunded.

## Business plan refunds

On Business, every refund within 30 days of the charge is reviewed by our team. Support prepares the request with the charge amount from our billing record, and a person approves or rejects it.

A Business charge outside the refund window cannot be refunded.

## Enterprise refunds

On Enterprise, every refund is decided by our team under the terms of your contract. Support prepares the request and opens a Pending approval ticket. There is no automatic refund on Enterprise.

## Past-due accounts

Refunds cannot be issued while an account is past due, because the account has an unpaid balance. When you ask for a refund on a past-due account, we pass the request to our billing team, who will follow up with you.

A payment grace extension does not change this: the account stays past due until the balance is paid.

## Cancelled and in-notice accounts

- **Cancelled:** a recent charge can still be refunded under the normal rules for your plan.
- **In notice:** the normal rules for your plan apply until the end of the term.
